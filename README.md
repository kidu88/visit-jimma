# Visit Jimma

Welcome to **Visit Jimma**, a web-based platform designed to promote the historical, cultural, and natural attractions of Jimma, Ethiopia.

The platform helps visitors explore Jimma's famous landmarks, experience its rich coffee culture, discover traditional clothing and local food, and plan their visits through convenient booking features.

Our goal is to make discovering and experiencing Jimma easier, more accessible, and enjoyable for both local and international visitors.

## Key Features

- **Discover Attractions:** Explore historical landmarks, tourist destinations, and beautiful natural attractions in Jimma.
- **Explore Local Culture:** Learn about traditional Ethiopian clothing, local cuisine, and Jimma's famous coffee culture.
- **Booking System:** Access booking features for organizing visits and accommodation reservations.
- **User Authentication:** Register and log in to manage user accounts.
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy a user-friendly interface across different screen sizes.
